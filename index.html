Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers to student, parent, and owner questions about our verification protocol and safeguards.

Our full-time audit team physically visits each PG with calibrated wide-aperture cameras. We record an unedited, continuous walkthrough starting from the main street entrance, security desk, stairwells, hallways, bedroom, attached washroom, RO water point, and the food kitchen. The footage is geo-tagged and time-stamped so you see exactly what natural lighting, ceiling condition, and bathroom water pressure look like in real life.
If you arrive at the PG and find that the room, washroom, bed allocation, or amenities substantially differ from the video walkthrough recorded by Placely, report it within 72 hours of move-in. Your initial booking fee will be promptly refunded, and our team will help relocate you to an audited alternative.
PG properties change rapidly—paint peels, washroom plumbing breaks down, and kitchen cooks get replaced. A verification that is six months old cannot be trusted. By automatically expiring the Verified badge at 180 days, we force a mandatory re-visit and re-filming. At 170–179 days, the listing displays “Renewal Due”, and at 180+ days it displays “Re-verification Due (Expired)”. Expired PGs never look verified.
Many PG owners quote low rent and later demand mandatory meal surcharges, maintenance fees, and inflated electricity rates. On Placely, every single recurring and one-time charge is audited and locked. The True Monthly Cost formula is: Rent + Food + Electricity. By signing onto Placely, the property owner legally commits that anything not listed on the video audit card cannot be demanded from the student.
No. We never hold student money. All booking transactions are routed through an RBI-regulated and licensed payment gateway partner directly into verified merchant escrow accounts. This eliminates fraud risk and ensures full banking trace records.
Placely operates with a strict 72-hour complaint resolution target. If a PG receives two valid, verified complaints within a single calendar month (e.g. unhygienic meals or non-functioning water geysers), its Verified badge is immediately suspended and an unannounced on-site re-audit is triggered.
